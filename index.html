My printer appears in the iPhone print sheet

Use AirPrint. Before you confirm, open the printer options and select Actual Size or 100% if your printer provides that control. Turn off any setting that scales the page to fit. Apple’s AirPrint guide explains the iPhone print flow.

My printer does not appear in the print sheet

Export the PDF from StencilFit, then share it to a printer companion app that supports PDF imports, or move it to a computer to print. Check that app or computer’s print dialog for Actual Size or 100% and disable automatic fitting.

How do I know the size is right?

Print the matching calibration sheet at Actual Size or 100%, then measure the reference line with a ruler. If it is off, correct the printer’s scaling before printing your stencil.

Can every printer produce exact output?

Printer software, margins, paper handling, and automatic scaling vary. StencilFit prepares the layout, while the calibration check verifies your own printer and chosen print path.
