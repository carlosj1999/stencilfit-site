StencilFit Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

StencilFit is a tattoo stencil layout and printing utility.

Data collection

StencilFit does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data.

Images and files

Images imported into StencilFit are processed locally on your device for layout, sizing, PDF export, and printing. StencilFit does not upload imported designs to a server.

Accounts and analytics

StencilFit does not use user accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Purchases

StencilFit offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions (StencilFit Pro, monthly or annual). Purchase, payment, renewal, refund, and promotional offer-code processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings. StencilFit reads Apple's signed entitlement information only to determine whether Pro features should be available; it does not receive or store payment-card information. Customers who purchased StencilFit before it switched to subscriptions keep permanent Pro access.

Terms of Use

StencilFit uses Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA), available at apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula.
